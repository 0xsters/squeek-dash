How it works

  • Every few seconds the game reads the public Squeekchain status API: wheel state, RPM, rim speed in millimetres per second, and the current Squeek block height.
  • His rim speed sets your world speed. A sleeping hamster means a gentle jog, a hamster at full zoomies means you had better be awake too.
  • The wheel in the corner spins at his real RPM. The race bars compare your distance with the distance his wheel covers during your run.
  • No wallet, no signup, no gas. Scores live only in your browser. If the signal drops, the game keeps the last pace and tells you.
